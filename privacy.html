Privacy policy

Peak Clipesh Publisher ("the app") is used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channels and to read statistics about those videos.

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies.

What the app accesses

Through Google OAuth the app gets permission to upload videos to, and read channel data of, the owner's channels (scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly).

What the app stores

Only video ids, links, publishing times and view, like and comment counts of the owner's own videos, in a private database. The app collects no data about viewers or other YouTube users and shares no data with third parties.

Retention and deletion

Authorisation is re-checked at least every 30 days. Stored data is deleted within 7 days of a deletion request sent to the contact address below. Access can be revoked at any time at Google's security settings; data for a revoked channel is deleted within 7 days.
